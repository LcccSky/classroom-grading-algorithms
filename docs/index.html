2. Six Statistical Exam Curving Formulas & Inverse Cutoffs

University STEM exams frequently require post-exam curving. When an instructor applies a Square-Root curve (f(x) = 10√x) to a 75-point midterm, writing raw point cutoffs on the whiteboard requires the exact monotonic inverse g⁻¹(T) = T² / 100 (so an A- at 90% requires 60.8 / 75 pts rather than 67.5 pts).
